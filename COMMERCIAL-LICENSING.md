## When you need a commercial license

AGPL-3.0 § 13 requires that if you modify Hush Engine and make it available over a network (for example, inside a web service), you must provide the complete source code of your modified version to users of that service.

You need a commercial license if any of the following apply:

- You are building a **proprietary product** that links to Hush Engine and you do not want to distribute your code under AGPL.
- You are operating a **SaaS or hosted service** that uses Hush Engine and you do not want AGPL § 13 to apply to the rest of your stack.
- Your company's legal policy **prohibits AGPL** code in internal or customer-facing systems.
- You want a **commercial support or warranty** agreement alongside the software.

If you are using Hush Engine for non-commercial research, a personal project, internal tooling you do not distribute, or an open source project that is compatible with AGPL-3.0, you do not need a commercial license.
